Before You Call

What to Have Ready

Have the street address and the town ready, plus a short description of what is wrong and where. Tell us if water is near outlets, if sewage is involved or if anyone in the home is elderly or unwell. Mention the age of the building and any recent plumbing work if you know it.

If you can safely do so, locate the main water shutoff before you call. In many older Maple Shade homes it is in the basement near the front wall or by the meter. Our burst pipe page explains what to do first.
