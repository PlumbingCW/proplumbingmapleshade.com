What a Proper Water Heater Install Covers

A good installation is more than swapping a tank. Venting, gas connections, the relief valve, shutoffs and the expansion tank all have to meet code and fit the building. We walk through each item and explain what has to change before we quote.

We check space, fuel, venting, and the existing piping.

3

Set, vent, connect and add the expansion tank and relief line.

4

Test

Fill, fire, check for leaks and set the temperature.

Capacity follows how many people use hot water at once. A tank that is too small runs out. One that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot.

The Expansion Tank and Relief Valve

When water heats, it expands. In a closed plumbing system, with a check valve or pressure reducing valve at the meter, that pressure has nowhere to go. An expansion tank absorbs it and protects the heater and fixtures. We check whether your system is closed and whether the existing tank is waterlogged.

The temperature and pressure relief valve is a safety device, and its discharge line has to run to a safe spot. We never cap or plug one, and we replace a valve that looks corroded.

Permits, Code and Licensing in New Jersey

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed master plumber or authorized plumbing contractor. The state's Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode is based on the National Standard Plumbing Code. Whether your job needs a permit depends on the work, so ask the Maple Shade Construction Office at 200 Stiles Avenue.

Gas appliance work has its own safety rules. We do not commit to a schedule where a gas or electrical inspection is required.

Installing in Maple Shade Homes and Apartments

Maple Shade's housing stock grew quickly between 1960 and 1980, according to its 2006 master plan. Utility closets in apartments and townhomes from that period are often tight, and old tanks may sit on shallow drain pans with little venting clearance. We measure first so a new unit fits.

The township's water comes from its own wells and from New Jersey American Water, and Maple Shade Utilities reports that it treats high iron. Sediment of any kind shortens tank life, so we recommend a periodic flush for whatever tank we install.

For a failed tank see water heater replacement, and for a unit that is still serviceable, water heater repair. We also install in Moorestown and Mount Laurel.

Planning the Day of the Install

Clear a path to the heater, move stored items away and tell us about parking. The water will be off for a few hours, and the old tank has to be drained first. Plan showers and laundry around the visit.

Afterward the new tank needs time to heat. A standard tank can take an hour or more to reach temperature. We set the thermostat and show you where the shutoff valves are.
