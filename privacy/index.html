Privacy

Information We Collect

This site has no forms and no accounts, so we do not collect names or addresses through the website. Information reaches us only when you call or email us.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

Third Parties

Maps, Fonts and Hosting

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map. Those requests send your IP address and browser details to Google under its own privacy policy. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

We use your phone number and the details you give us only to schedule and perform plumbing work and to follow up on it. We do not sell personal information.
