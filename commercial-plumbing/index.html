When a Plumbing Failure Closes the Doors

A business with no working restrooms or a flooding back room cannot stay open, and our emergency commercial plumbing line in Maple Shade is open 24/7 with same-day visits when we can. Shut the main or the zone valve, move stock and equipment away from the water and tell customers if you need to close.

Give us the building type and who controls the plumbing. In a shared building the landlord may own the main lines, and we ask early so the right person approves the work.

Restaurants, Retail and Offices Along Route 38 and Route 73

Maple Shade's master plan identifies the Route 38 and Route 73 corridors as commercial areas and Main Street as the business center. A strip shop, a diner and an office suite have very different plumbing, so the first question we ask is about the building and its use.

Restaurants depend on grease control. A grease interceptor that is not maintained overloads the drain, and a kitchen line that backs up closes the kitchen. Hydro jetting on a regular schedule is often cheaper than a Saturday night emergency.

Neighboring commercial districts in Pennsauken include industrial parks, and we ask about the age and layout of those buildings as well.

Planned Work and Preventive Visits

Emergency calls are the smaller part of commercial plumbing. Regular drain cleaning, water heater checks and backflow device inspections keep a business from losing a day. We can schedule visits around opening hours so work does not interrupt customers.

Backflow testing rules for commercial properties vary, so ask your water utility and the Construction Office which devices need testing. We will explain what we can and cannot test.

Landlords, Tenants and Property Managers

Roughly half of Maple Shade households rent, and many businesses lease their space too. Lease terms decide who pays for plumbing inside a unit and who pays for the building's main lines. We document the cause of a failure in writing so the right party can approve the repair.

Property managers can give us building access details and an authorized contact at the start of the call, and that speeds up an after-hours visit.

Building a Relationship Before an Emergency

The best time to give us the details of your building is before a failure. Know where the main shutoff, the water heater, the floor drains and the cleanouts are. Tell your staff and post the 24/7 number where managers can find it.

We can walk a building on a planned visit and note the age and condition of fixtures, water heaters and drains. That list tells you what is likely to fail next and saves time when something does.
