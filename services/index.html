Emergency and Planned Work

Pick the Problem You Have

Most calls fall into two groups. Emergency work such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters gets the 24/7 line and the next available visit. Planned work such as installs, repiping and camera inspections can be scheduled around your week.

Not sure which page fits? Start with the symptom. A wet ceiling points toward leak detection, a gurgling floor drain points toward sewer line cleaning, and a cold shower usually starts with water heater repair. If you cannot tell, call and describe it.

How We Work

What Every Service Visit Includes

Each visit starts with a short diagnosis so the price is based on what is actually wrong. You hear a clear price before work starts, and the work does not begin until you agree. When the repair is finished we test it and tell you what to watch for.

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor. We work under that rule, and permits where a job needs them are handled through the Maple Shade Construction Office.
