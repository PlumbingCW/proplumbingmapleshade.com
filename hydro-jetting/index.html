Backed-Up Line That Will Not Stay Clear

A line that backs up with grease or scale is an emergency when the building cannot use its drains. Our emergency hydro jetting line in Maple Shade is open 24/7, and we book same-day visits when we can. Keep water use to a minimum, avoid chemical cleaners and tell us if the line has been cleaned recently.

Jetting is not the first tool for every clog. It is the right one when a cable only opens a small hole in a coated pipe.

How Hydro Jetting Works

1

Camera first

We look inside to confirm the pipe is sound enough for jetting.

2

Nozzle in

A hose with a rotating nozzle goes through the cleanout.

3

Scour

Water at high pressure strips grease, scale and soft roots from the walls.

4

Check the result

A second camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

The water flushes debris to the main as the nozzle backs out. Done properly, it leaves the pipe closer to its original diameter than a cable does.

Jetting Versus Cabling

Cable machine

  • Cuts a path through a clog
  • Quick and effective for roots and solid blockage
  • Can leave a coating on the wall
  • Regrowth possible sooner

Hydro jetting

  • Scours the whole pipe wall
  • Best for grease, scale and heavy buildup
  • Needs a sound pipe
  • Often keeps the line clear longer

When Jetting Is a Poor Idea

Jetting is not for broken, offset or collapsed pipe, because high pressure can worsen the damage. Very old or fragile pipe may be a poor candidate as well. That is why we start with a camera and tell you plainly when cabling or repair is the safer choice.

If the camera finds a defect, see sewer line repair. For a routine clog without a history of returns, drain cleaning may be all you need.

Restaurants, Shops and Multifamily Buildings

Maple Shade's master plan identifies Route 38, Route 73 and Main Street as commercial centers, and the township also has many apartment buildings from the 1960 to 1980 growth period. Kitchens in restaurants and the shared stacks of apartment buildings send heavy grease and soap into pipes every day. Those are the lines where jetting pays off.

If you manage a building, schedule jetting before a backup instead of after. A planned visit is easier on tenants and customers than a midnight call. See our commercial plumbing page for business service.

Jetting Frequency and Maintenance

How often a line needs jetting depends on what goes into it. A restaurant kitchen line may need service several times a year. A household line with one grease clog may need it once and never again. We recommend an interval based on what the camera showed.

Between visits, pour grease into a can instead of the sink, scrape plates into the trash and use strainers in kitchen and shower drains. These small habits stretch the time between jobs.

What You Will See on the Day

We set up at the cleanout, protect the floor and run the camera first. The jetting itself is loud for a few minutes. When the nozzle is pulled back, water pushes debris toward the main, so expect water to run in the drain while it works.

Afterward we run the camera again and show you the cleaned pipe. If we see damage, we explain it and leave the decision to you.
