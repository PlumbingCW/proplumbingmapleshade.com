Coverage

How Dispatch Works Across Township Lines

The 24/7 line is one number for every town. When you call, give the town and the street, because that tells us which direction the technician will come from and which office issues permits if the job needs one.

Route 38 and Route 73 connect Maple Shade to most of these towns, and I-295 and the New Jersey Turnpike cover the longer runs to Cherry Hill and Mount Laurel. Traffic and weather change drive times, so we give a window on the call and not a promise.

Inside Maple Shade

Neighborhoods and Districts

Maple Shade is small, at under four square miles, and it has no documented named subdivisions that residents widely use. Two parts of town are distinct enough for their own pages. Residents near the center can read about downtown Maple Shade and Main Street. Shops and buildings on the highways can read about the Route 38 and Route 73 corridors.
