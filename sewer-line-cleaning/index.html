Main Line Backup: Stop the Water, Then Call

A blocked main sewer line is an emergency because nothing you flush or drain has anywhere to go. Our emergency sewer cleaning line in Maple Shade is open 24/7, and we book same-day visits when we can. Stop running water, hold off on laundry and dishes, and keep people away from any floor drain that is overflowing.

If you can find a cleanout, a capped pipe usually near the foundation or in the basement, leave it closed until the plumber is there. Opening one under pressure releases sewage onto the floor.

What Causes a Main Line to Block

Tree roots

Fine roots enter joints and grow into a mat that catches paper and waste.

Grease and wipes

Grease coats the walls, and wipes labeled flushable do not break down.

A sag in the pipe

A low section holds water and solids, a problem a camera shows.

Collapse or offset

A broken pipe stops flow completely and needs repair, not cleaning.

Cleaning removes what is in the pipe. It does not fix the pipe, so if a line blocks again within a few months we recommend a camera pass before a third cleaning.

After the Line Is Open

Once water flows, we run a camera through the cleaned section to confirm it is clear and look for damage. If we see cracks, roots at joints or a belly, we show you the footage and discuss repair choices. You are never obliged to repair anything the same day.

To keep the line open, avoid pouring grease down the drain, skip flushable wipes and keep tree roots in mind when planting near the line. An annual camera check or cleaning is reasonable for a line with a history of roots.

Keeping a Cleaned Line Clear

Cleaning is not a permanent fix if the cause stays. Grease poured down kitchen drains, wipes labeled flushable and roots entering joints bring the clog back. Cleanouts that are easy to reach make future cleaning cheaper.

If you know your line has roots, ask about a yearly cleaning schedule. A scheduled visit costs less than an emergency visit and keeps your plans intact.
