Disposal Leaking or Jammed: Do This First

A leaking disposal can soak the cabinet and floor quickly, and a jammed unit keeps a kitchen out of use. Our emergency garbage disposal repair line in Maple Shade is open 24/7, and we book same-day visits when we can. Turn the disposal off at the wall switch, and if you are going to clear it, unplug it or turn off its breaker first.

Never put a hand in the chamber. If it hums but does not spin, it is jammed. If water is spraying, close the shutoff valves under the sink.

Is It the Disposal or the Drain?

A disposal that runs fine but fills the sink is not the problem. Water backs up when the drain beyond it is clogged, usually with grease. A cable or jetter clears it, and a new disposal would not help.

If the second sink bowl also fills, or other fixtures gurgle, the clog is further down the line. Hold off on running the disposal until it is cleared.

Installing a New Disposal

A replacement involves removing the old unit, mounting the new one on the sink flange and reconnecting the drain and wiring. Horsepower is a practical choice. Half horsepower handles a light household, and larger units suit heavy use. We also check the drain connection and the dishwasher hose, since both attach to the disposal.

The wiring is usually a plug or a hardwired connection, and local code covers how it is installed. Ask the Maple Shade Construction Office if a permit is needed for your situation.

Disposal Habits That Prevent Calls

Run cold water before, during and for a few seconds after grinding. Feed scraps in small amounts. Avoid grease, fibrous vegetables like celery, large bones and anything that expands in water, such as rice or pasta in quantity.

Citrus peels and ice cubes freshen the chamber and are fine in small amounts. Chemical drain cleaners are not. They damage seals and put the next person who works on the line at risk.
