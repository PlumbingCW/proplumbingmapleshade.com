The Township That Grew Overnight

Cherry Hill was incorporated in 1844 as Delaware Township and renamed in 1961. Its population went from 10,358 in 1950 to 31,522 in 1960 and 64,395 in 1970. At about 24 square miles and 74,553 residents in the 2020 Census, it is the largest township we serve.

That growth means a very large number of homes share a construction era. Houses from the 1950s and 1960s are now past their original water heaters, shutoffs and supply lines, and the plumbing tends to age together.

History and Roads, and Why They Matter Here

Barclay Farm House dates to 1816 and Croft Farm to 1753, and the Cherry Hill Mall opened in 1961. Those landmarks sit among newer development, so the township mixes very old buildings with typical postwar housing. A farmhouse and a split-level need different plumbing thinking.

Routes 70, 38 and 41 are the main roads, and our office is on Route 38. Southwest of Maple Shade, Cherry Hill is about six miles away. We give a window for arrival, not a promise.

What to Do When the Water Looks Different

Water mains age, and projects like the Kresson Road replacement can stir sediment or briefly change pressure nearby. If water runs brown, run a cold tap in the lowest part of the house for several minutes. If it clears, the cause was likely in the street.

Check faucet aerators for grit afterward and clean them. If the discoloration comes only from hot taps, the water heater may be the source, since sediment collects in the tank. If pressure stays low in the whole house, call and describe it. We check your side of the shutoff and tell you when the cause is in the street.

Finished Basements and What Water Does to Them

Many Cherry Hill homes have finished basements, and finished space turns a small leak into an expensive one. Carpet, drywall and stored belongings absorb water quickly, and mold can start within a couple of days.

Know where your floor drain, sump pit and main shutoff are, and keep stored items off the floor. If water appears, shut off the main, stop the source and photograph everything before moving it. We stop the plumbing problem, and a drying contractor handles the rest.
