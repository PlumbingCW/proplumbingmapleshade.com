Failed Tank: Stop the Flood

A tank that has failed can release dozens of gallons, and a house without hot water is a hardship. Our emergency water heater replacement line in Maple Shade is open 24/7, and we book same-day visits when we can. Close the cold-water valve at the top of the tank, switch off the power at the breaker or set the gas valve to off, and open a hot tap upstairs to let the tank drain.

A tank that has leaked from its shell cannot be repaired. We confirm the failure and plan the replacement on the same visit when we can.

Repair or Replace?

SituationUsual answer
Tank leaking from the shellReplace
Tank older than about 12 years with repeated repairsReplace
Failed element or thermostat on a young tankRepair, see water heater repair
Rust-colored hot water onlyCheck the anode rod and flush first
Tank too small for the householdReplace with a larger size

Replacement is not always a like-for-like swap. We check venting, gas line size, the relief valve and the expansion tank to confirm the new unit meets code.

Choosing the Replacement

Standard tank

  • Lower purchase price
  • Simple swap in most homes
  • Stores 30 to 80 gallons
  • Fits existing venting

Tankless

  • Continuous hot water
  • Needs a gas or electrical upgrade
  • Often a larger install
  • See tankless water heaters

Size matters as much as type. A family of four usually needs a larger tank than a single person. We ask about peak use, such as two showers and a dishwasher at once.

Replacement in Maple Shade Homes

The 2006 master plan shows Maple Shade added thousands of units between 1970 and 1980, and tanks from that era are long gone. The question is usually the second or third tank. Check the date on the data label. If yours is near 10 years old, plan the replacement before the failure, since a planned job saves a night without hot water.

Apartments often have the tank in a closet with limited clearance, so we measure before we order. We also replace tanks in Cherry Hill and Cinnaminson.

These are starting points. First-hour rating and recovery rate matter as much as the label gallons. We choose with you and explain the numbers.

Signs Your Tank Is Near the End

Tanks give warning signs. Rust-colored hot water, a rumbling sound, small puddles at the base and a unit older than 10 years are all signs the tank is aging. Planning the replacement before it fails lets you pick the unit, check venting and avoid a night without hot water.

Look at the data label for the date code, which is usually printed in the serial number. If you cannot read it, tell us the brand and we can help decode it on the call.

Insurance note

A sudden tank rupture that damages the home may be covered. The tank itself, as normal wear, usually is not.

Code referenceA water heater swap is covered by the state plumbing subcode (the National Standard Plumbing Code, adopted in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15), and gas or venting changes have their own rules. Ask the Maple Shade Construction Office whether your swap needs a permit and inspection.
