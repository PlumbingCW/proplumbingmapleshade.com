No Hot Water: What to Check Before You Call

A dead water heater is an emergency when it leaks or when a household cannot go without hot water. Our emergency water heater repair line in Maple Shade is open 24/7, and we book same-day visits when we can. If water is pooling, close the cold-water valve on top of the tank and, for an electric unit, switch off its breaker.

1

Check the power or pilot

Electric units need a tripped breaker reset. Gas units need a lit pilot and the gas valve open.

2

Feel the pipes

A hot outlet pipe with no hot water at the tap points to a fixture or mixing valve.

3

Look at the base

Water around the tank can be a fitting, which is repairable, or a failed tank, which is not.

4

Note the data label

Brand, age and capacity let us arrive with the right parts.

A Water Heater Leak Is Not Always the Tank

Leaks cause a large share of the water heater calls we see, and many of them come from fittings, not the tank. Supply connections, the relief valve, the drain valve and the anode port can all weep. Those are repairable, and finding the true source keeps a sound tank in service.

A tank that leaks from its shell has corroded through and cannot be patched. Water also reaches the floor from a nearby pipe or a condensation line, so we trace the water before we name the cause.

Maple Shade Water and Your Tank

The 2024 water quality report from Maple Shade Utilities says the township treats high iron in water from its wells before it reaches customers. Iron or any sediment that does arrive settles at the bottom of a tank, which is why an annual flush is worth doing. We do not state a hardness figure for Maple Shade because the report does not give one, and a simple test of your own tap is the better guide.

Gas Versus Electric: Where Failures Differ

Gas and electric tanks fail in different places. On a gas unit, the thermocouple, pilot assembly, gas control valve and flue are the usual suspects. On an electric unit, the upper and lower heating elements and the thermostats fail far more often than the tank.

That difference matters for repair cost. Replacing one element on an electric tank is a modest job, while a failed gas control valve on an older tank may cost enough that replacement is the better value. We tell you where your unit falls before we quote.

If you smell gas near a gas tank, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Do not call us from inside the house.

Temperature, Scalding and Safety

Many safety guides suggest setting a tank to about 120 degrees Fahrenheit, which reduces scald risk, especially in homes with children or older adults. If your water is scalding or never gets hot, the thermostat or a mixing valve may be at fault. We check the set point and the actual output.

The temperature and pressure relief valve is a safety device. If it drips or discharges, treat it as a warning, not a nuisance. Never cap the discharge pipe.
