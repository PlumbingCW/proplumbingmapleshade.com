Cleaning Drains With Baking Soda and Vinegar: Does It Work?

The kitchen-cupboard method is popular and harmless. Here is what it can freshen, what it cannot unclog and when to stop.

By the Maple Shade Pro Plumbing team · Published November 12, 2026 · Plumbing guide for Maple Shade, NJ homeowners

The Short Answer

Key points

  • Baking soda and vinegar can freshen a drain and loosen a thin film, but they will not clear a solid clog.
  • Hair clogs respond better to a strainer, a hair puller or a hand snake.
  • Skip chemical cleaners, which can damage pipes and endanger whoever works on the line next.
  • Several slow drains at once point to the main line, not a fixture.
  • A repeat clog calls for a camera, not a third bottle of cleaner.

What Happens in the Pipe

Baking soda is a mild base, and vinegar is a mild acid. Together they fizz and produce carbon dioxide, water and a salt. The fizz can loosen a thin film of soap and grease near the drain opening and freshen a mild odor.

What it cannot do is break up a solid clog. Hair, a wad of paper or a plug of congealed grease sits farther down the pipe, and a few minutes of foam does not reach or dissolve it.

A Method That Is Safe to Try

1

Clear the standing water

Remove what you can with a cup so the mixture reaches the pipe.

2

Add baking soda

Pour about half a cup down the drain.

3

Add vinegar

Follow with a cup of white vinegar and cover the drain.

4

Flush with hot water

After 15 minutes, flush with hot tap water, not boiling water on PVC.

Use it for slow drains and odors, and expect a small improvement. If nothing changes after one or two tries, move on to a plunger or a plumber.

What Works Better for Real Clogs

ClogBetter option
Hair in a tub or showerDrain strainer, hair puller, hand snake
Soap and grease in a kitchen sinkHot water flush, then a plunger
Toilet blockageFlange plunger or closet auger
Several drains at oncePlumber, since the main line is likely blocked

A hand-cranked snake from a hardware store handles hair clogs close to the fixture. Do not push it hard, which can scratch a pipe or push the clog further down.

Skip the Chemical Cleaners

Commercial drain cleaners can damage pipes and fixtures, and they are hazardous if they splash. If one sits in a blocked line, the plumber who arrives later has to work around it. Tell us if you have used one so we can wear the right protection.

Enzyme cleaners are slower but gentler, and they suit routine maintenance more than emergencies.

Local sourceMaple Shade's 2006 master plan documents rapid housing growth in the 1960s and 1970s, which means many drain stacks are now several decades old. Older drains of cast iron roughen inside as they age, so a household method that once worked may stop helping as the pipe wall becomes rough.

Prevention That Actually Works

Use strainers in sinks and showers, wipe greasy pans before washing them and pour cooled grease into a can. Flush a drain with hot water once a week to keep soap film from building.

In kitchens, run the disposal with plenty of cold water. In bathrooms, brush hair out of the stopper regularly. These steps take seconds and save a service call.

Signs the Problem Is Deeper Than a Fixture

A drain that clears and slows again within days, a gurgle from another fixture or a bad smell that returns all point beyond the trap. A plumber can run a camera to see what is happening, and a short visit often costs less than months of home remedies.

If sewage or gray water is backing up, stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

A Simple Kitchen Routine

After washing dishes, run hot water for 30 seconds. Once a week, pour hot water with a little dish soap down the sink to cut grease. Once a month, try the baking soda and vinegar method for freshness.

That routine will not clear a hard clog. It keeps a healthy drain healthy, and it is better than waiting for a slowdown.

What a Plumber Brings That a Kitchen Cabinet Does Not

Cable machines reach far down the line. Cameras show the cause. Jetters scour the pipe wall. These are the tools that clear what a household method cannot, and they work in minutes instead of days.

If you have tried the home methods twice and the drain is still slow, it is time to call.

When to Call a Plumber

Call when more than one drain is slow, when water backs up in a floor drain or when the same clog returns. Those point to a main line problem that no household remedy reaches. See sewer line cleaning for what happens next.

If a kitchen line keeps clogging with grease, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall. We take calls from Pennsauken and Cinnaminson too.
