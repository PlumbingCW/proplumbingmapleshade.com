Two Highways, Two Kinds of Calls

The 2006 Maple Shade master plan names the Route 38 and Route 73 corridors as the places the township wants to focus commercial and industrial redevelopment. These are business strips, not residential neighborhoods, and we treat them that way here.

Our own address, 451 Route 38, is on the same corridor. That is why a call from a shop or office on either highway is a short trip. Route 73 also carries on into Mount Laurel and Pennsauken, so the same strip crosses township lines.

What Commercial Calls Look Like

A restaurant with a backed-up floor drain is a different call than a house with a slow sink. A closed kitchen costs a day of business, so we ask when you open, what you serve and how many fixtures are affected. We then tell you what to stop using.

Offices and retail units usually call about restrooms, water heaters and leaks above ceilings. Tenants in a strip center often share lines with neighbors, so we ask who owns the building and who can approve work before we arrive.

Before We Arrive: Shops and Restaurants

Do these while you wait

  • Shut off the fixture valve or the water to the affected area. Ask the building owner where the main shutoff is.
  • Stop using sinks, floor drains and restrooms that are backing up, and put out signs if customers are affected.
  • Keep clear of wet outlets, panels and kitchen equipment.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into a backed-up line.
  • Photograph the water and the damage for the owner and the insurer.

Services for Commercial Strips

Our commercial plumbing page lists what we handle for shops, offices and restaurants. Grease and heavy use are why hydro jetting and sewer line cleaning come up for food service. When the cause is unclear, a sewer camera inspection shows what is in the line.

Businesses with tank water heaters often ask about water heater replacement before a failure closes the restrooms or kitchen.

Creeks, Drainage and Apartment Communities

The town history notes an early sawmill on Pennsauken Creek where Mill Road crosses Route 38, and the master plan says flood plains and stream corridors are mapped and considered in development review. The plan also calls for a study of storm drainage problems. If your lot is near a creek, ask the township about drainage on your street. We do not claim any one strip floods.

Several apartment communities are listed with Route 73 and Kings Highway addresses, including Village of Stoney Run and the Watergate condominiums. Residents of those communities should start with their property manager. Managers and owners are welcome to call us about building plumbing.

Permits and Next Steps

Whether a job needs a permit depends on the work. Ask the Maple Shade Construction Office at 200 Stiles Avenue, and we will tell you what the job involves. For residential calls in the center of town, see downtown Maple Shade or the main Maple Shade plumber page.
