Remodel Plumbing: Get It Right at the Start

A remodel is the cheapest time to fix plumbing, because the walls are open. Old shutoff valves, brittle supply lines and undersized drains can be replaced at a fraction of the usual cost. We walk the job with your contractor or on our own, and we tell you what to replace while it is easy.

Moving a toilet or shower changes the drain and vent, which is real work and often needs a permit. Ask the Maple Shade Construction Office at 200 Stiles Avenue about permit rules for your project.

Code and Licensing

New Jersey law requires plumbing work to be performed by a master plumber or an authorized plumbing contractor. The Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode is based on the National Standard Plumbing Code. We install to that baseline.

Ask us about the code points for your project, such as vent sizing or shutoffs. If a point is not ours to decide, we will point you to the Construction Office.

Installing in Older Maple Shade Homes

Maple Shade grew quickly between 1960 and 1980 per the 2006 master plan, and many homes have their original supply lines, drains and shutoffs. When we add a fixture to an older house, we test pressure and look at the drain capacity first. A new fixture on a tired line is a recipe for a callback.

Rentals are about half of local households. Landlords and property managers often ask us for documented work, and we can describe what was installed. We serve Mount Laurel and Moorestown homeowners with larger remodels as well.

Related Work

If you are replacing a failing water heater as part of a remodel, see water heater installation. Old supply lines may call for repiping, and a failing faucet can be handled through faucet repair.

Bathroom and Kitchen Remodel Plumbing

A bathroom remodel typically involves a toilet, vanity, tub or shower, and sometimes a relocated drain. A kitchen involves the sink, faucet, disposal, dishwasher and possibly a pot filler or ice maker line. We coordinate the plumbing steps with the rough-in and the finish so nothing waits on us.

Choosing fixtures before the rough-in helps. Rough-in dimensions vary by model, and the wrong choice can mean opening a finished wall to move a valve.
