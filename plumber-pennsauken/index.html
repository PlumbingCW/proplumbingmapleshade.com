Pennsauken Calls We Handle Most

Older housing and a lot of drainage infrastructure make sewer and drain problems the headline here. When several fixtures back up at once, we start with sewer line cleaning and, if the line keeps blocking, a camera inspection. Slow kitchen drains come next, and drain cleaning covers them.

After that come water heaters and leaks, then business calls from the industrial park and the shops along the highways, which fall under commercial plumbing.

Safety Steps

Before We Arrive: Backups and Floods

Do these while you wait

  • Stop using drains if sewage or gray water is rising, and keep people away from it.
  • Close the main shutoff for a burst or leaking supply line.
  • Do not run the washer or dishwasher while the drains are blocked.
  • Photograph standing water for your records.

Rentals, Multi-Unit Buildings and Industrial Calls

With 13,275 housing units counted in 2010 and neighborhoods packed close together, Pennsauken has many rented homes and small multifamily buildings. When a leak starts, the first question is who authorizes the work. Tell your landlord or property manager at once, and give us their contact if they want a written description of the cause.

Businesses in the large industrial park and along the highways have a different checklist. Tell us what the building is used for, where the shutoffs and floor drains are and whether work can happen during business hours. After-hours visits are available when something cannot wait, and planned visits cost less than emergencies.

Questions to Ask Any Plumber About a Sewer Backup

A backup is stressful, and it is a good moment to ask questions. Ask whether the plumber will run a camera, since cleaning without looking is a guess. Ask how the blockage will be cleared, whether the line will be checked afterward and what the price covers before work starts.

Also ask who owns the blocked section. If the clog is in the public main, the sewerage authority fixes it. If it is in your lateral, a camera recording protects you in either case. We answer all of these on the call and again on site.

Getting Across the County Line

Our office is just west of the Maple Shade line on Route 38, and Pennsauken is a short drive toward Route 73 and Route 130. Airport Circle, which dates to 1927, and the Pennsauken Transit Center sit on the busier routes. The Betsy Ross Bridge connects Pennsauken to Philadelphia, which matters for traffic when the bridge backs up.

Nearby places like Merchantville and Camden are close by, and we cover the Pennsauken side of those borders. Neighboring towns we also serve include Cinnaminson and Cherry Hill.
